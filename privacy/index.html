CalSnap Privacy Policy

Last revised: October 28, 2026

CalSnap helps people track meals and calorie estimates. This policy explains how CalSnap handles local device data, authentication and security data, AI photo processing, and subscription information. CalSnap Build 17 does not offer Cloud Backup, cloud synchronization, or cloud restore of your local tracking data.

Local device data

CalSnap stores the information you enter locally on your device so the app can work offline. This can include your name, goal, calorie target, confirmed meal history, reusable foods, settings, reminder preferences, and weight entries. Local data is not sent to CalSnap merely because you use the app. You can delete the local device copy from CalSnap's data controls or by removing the app. Deleting local data does not delete historical cloud backups, authentication records, subscription records, or server-side scan-count records.

Account data and AI photo processing

CalSnap uses Firebase Authentication and may create or restore an anonymous account in the background to authenticate AI scan requests and enforce scan limits. Restoring this authentication account does not restore your meals, profile, or other local tracking data. The account identifier is not displayed in the app. CalSnap also uses Firebase App Check to help verify that requests come from the app and protect the service from misuse. Authentication and security processing continue even though Cloud Backup has been removed.

When you choose a photo, it stays on your device until you confirm the scan. After confirmation, CalSnap sends the selected image and its file type through Google Cloud Functions for Firebase to OpenAI to estimate visible foods, portions, and nutrition. The Firebase request also includes authentication and App Check information. CalSnap does not explicitly include your Firebase account identifier, profile, weight, or meal history in the request to OpenAI.

CalSnap's backend does not save the photo to its database, include photo content in application logs, or attach it to meal history. The app attempts to delete its temporary photo file after scanning or cancellation; this does not delete your original gallery photo and is not a guarantee of cleanup after a crash or interruption. CalSnap submits photos to obtain the requested estimate. OpenAI may retain submitted content under its API data-retention and safety policies; provider security and abuse-monitoring rules also apply. CalSnap does not promise immediate provider deletion or zero retention.

AI-generated estimates may be inaccurate and should always be reviewed and edited before being saved. You can cancel before submitting the photo or choose manual meal entry instead. Once processing has started, cancellation or a timeout does not establish that a provider has deleted the submitted photo.

CalSnap uses scan limits to help prevent misuse and manage costs. Cloud Firestore stores scan-count records associated with your Firebase account identifier, including the date, count, and update timestamp. These records do not contain the photo and are not backups of your local tracking data. A new daily allowance does not itself delete older scan-count records. Application logs record scan completion or failure information and may include image byte counts and returned food counts, without photo content. No fixed retention period for these records or logs is promised here.

Cloud Backup removal and previously stored data

Build 17 does not upload your profile, confirmed meal history, reusable foods, settings, or weight entries for Cloud Backup or cloud synchronization, and cannot restore that data from the cloud. Your current tracking data remains on your device, separate from the online processing described above.

Older versions may have stored cloud-backup data in Google Cloud Firestore under a Firebase account identifier. Removing Cloud Backup does not itself delete any previously stored data. Such data may remain stored. Build 17 does not automatically delete those backups, and no automatic 30-day deletion period is promised here. Build 17 does not provide controls to manage or delete those older backups.

For historical cloud-backup deletion requests, contact koraapps.support@gmail.com. We need to verify that the request concerns your data and identify the associated account before deleting records. Anonymous accounts may be difficult to identify if access has been lost. Deleting a historical backup does not itself delete your Firebase Authentication account or cancel a subscription. Never send us passwords, authentication tokens, or other sign-in secrets.

Subscription and payment information

Where subscriptions are available, Apple App Store or Google Play processes payments. CalSnap and KoraApps do not receive or store your full payment-card details. RevenueCat processes purchase history, subscription status, and your Firebase account identifier to provide, manage, and verify Premium access. CalSnap uses that identifier as the RevenueCat app user ID. The app checks the configured active Premium entitlement, and the scan service independently checks RevenueCat to determine the applicable AI scan allowance. This check does not send the food photo to RevenueCat.

Restore Purchases attempts to restore eligible subscriptions associated with your store account, subject to store and RevenueCat restore rules. It does not restore local food history, profile, settings, other local tracking data, or historical cloud backups. Subscription availability and restoration depend on the build, store, and service configuration; successful restoration is not guaranteed.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact koraapps.support@gmail.com. We may update this policy as CalSnap changes; the latest version will show its revision date.

Calorie estimates are informational and are not medical advice. People with medical, dietary, or eating-disorder concerns should seek advice from a qualified clinician.
