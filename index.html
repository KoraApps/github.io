CalSnap Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026

CalSnap helps people track meals and calorie estimates. This policy explains how CalSnap handles local device data, optional account and cloud-backup data, AI photo processing, and subscription information.

Local device data

CalSnap stores the information you enter locally on your device so the app can work offline. This can include your name, goal, calorie target, confirmed meal history, reusable foods, settings, reminder preferences, and weight entries. Local data is not sent to CalSnap merely because you use the app. You can delete the local device copy from CalSnap's data controls or by removing the app.

Account data and AI photo processing

CalSnap may create or restore an anonymous Firebase account in the background to help protect the service and enforce AI scan limits. The account identifier is not displayed in the app and is used only for authentication, security, scan limits, and optional cloud backup. You may later choose to link this account to a sign-in method when that feature is available. If an anonymous account is not linked and the app is uninstalled or the account is lost, cloud-only data may no longer be accessible.

When you choose to scan a meal, CalSnap keeps the selected photo temporarily on your device until you confirm the scan. After you confirm, the photo is sent securely to our AI processing provider solely to estimate visible foods, portions, and nutritional information. We do not permanently store food photos, include them in application logs, or add them to meal history or cloud backup. Food photos are processed only for the requested scan.

AI-generated estimates may be inaccurate and should always be reviewed and edited before being saved. You can cancel the scan or choose manual meal entry at any time. During development, CalSnap limits AI scans to help prevent misuse and manage costs. CalSnap stores only the minimum information required to enforce scan limits and maintain security, and never the food photo itself.

Optional cloud backup data

Cloud backup is optional and is off by default. When you choose to enable it, CalSnap can securely back up your profile, confirmed meal history, reusable foods, settings, and weight entries using your Firebase account identifier. This information is stored in Europe in Google Cloud Firestore. Food photos are not included in cloud backup.

You can turn off cloud backup at any time. Turning it off stops future backup but does not remove the copy already stored in the cloud. You can request deletion of your active cloud-backup data through CalSnap's cloud-data controls or by contacting koraapps.support@gmail.com.

We delete active cloud-backup data as soon as reasonably possible after a verified deletion request. Residual copies can remain in backup or disaster-recovery systems for up to 30 days before permanent removal, where applicable. Food photos are never included in cloud backup or retained as meal-history data.

Subscription and payment information

If you choose to purchase a subscription after subscriptions are activated, Apple App Store or Google Play will process your payment. CalSnap and KoraApps do not receive or store your full payment-card details. RevenueCat may process purchase status and subscription identifiers to determine Premium access. This information is used to provide, restore, and manage subscription access.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact koraapps.support@gmail.com. We may update this policy as CalSnap changes; the latest version will show its effective date.

Calorie estimates are informational and are not medical advice. People with medical, dietary, or eating-disorder concerns should seek advice from a qualified clinician.
